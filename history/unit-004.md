## Key learning
- `Wh-word + do/does + subject + base verb?`
- `do` with I/you/we/they; `does` with he/she/it.
- The main verb stays in the base form after `does`.
- `live in + city/country/place`.
- `before work`, `after work`, `at work`.
- `in the morning`, `in the evening`, `at night`.
- British/New Zealand usage: `at the weekend`.
- `want to + verb`, e.g. `want to go`.
- `because` should introduce a complete reason clause.
